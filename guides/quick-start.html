Quick-Start Guide

You're 60 minutes away from your first professional SOP. This guide walks you through the fastest path from "I have no documentation" to "I have a real SOP I actually use."

Step 1: Pick Your First SOP (5 minutes)

Don't start with the most important process — start with the most painful one. Use this priority framework:

PriorityWhen to pick this processExample
Start hereYou've explained this process more than 3 timesHow to onboard a new client
HighMistakes here cost you real money or clientsQuality review before delivery
MediumThis process changes depending on who does itContent publishing workflow
LowerNice to have documented, but not hurting you yetInternal meeting notes format

Step 2: Choose Your Template (2 minutes)

Match the process to the right template:

TemplateBest forExamples
General SOPAny process that follows a linear flowClient onboarding, employee setup, monthly close
Client OnboardingBringing new clients from signed contract to activeAgency onboarding, consulting kickoff, service activation
Project DeliveryManaging a project from start to handoffWebsite build, campaign launch, product release
Content PublishingCreating and publishing content of any kindBlog posts, social media, email campaigns, reports
Quality AssuranceReview and approval processesDeliverable review, code review, design QA

Not sure? Start with the General SOP — it works for almost anything.

Step 3: Fill It In (30-45 minutes)

You have three options — pick the one that matches your style:

Option A: Fill in the Template Directly

  1. Open your chosen template
  2. Fill in each section — the template has placeholders and guidance for every field
  3. Your work saves automatically as you type
  4. When done, click "Save / Print PDF" to export

Option B: Use AI to Generate a Draft

  1. Open the SOP Generator prompts
  2. Copy Prompt 1 ("Generate a Complete SOP from Scratch")
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI tool
  4. Fill in your business details and run the prompt
  5. Copy the AI output into your template and edit to match reality

Option C: Convert Existing Notes

  1. Gather your existing notes, checklists, Slack messages, or brain dumps about the process
  2. Open the SOP Generator prompts
  3. Copy Prompt 2 ("Convert Messy Notes into a Clean SOP")
  4. Paste your notes and run the prompt
  5. Review and refine the output

Step 4: Validate It (10 minutes)

Before you call it done:

  1. Walk through it mentally. Read each step and imagine doing it. Does anything feel out of order or missing?
  2. Have someone else read it. If possible, ask a team member to follow it. Where do they get stuck or have questions?
  3. Use the Process Auditor. Copy your finished SOP into Process Auditor Prompt 1 for an AI-powered review.

Step 5: Put It to Work

Share with Your Team

  • Share the HTML file directly — it works offline in any browser, no special software needed
  • Click "Save / Print PDF" to export a clean PDF for email, Google Drive, or your team's document system
  • Each person who opens the file gets their own saved state — entries are stored locally in the browser
  • Set a calendar reminder to review and update quarterly

What to Build Next

After your first SOP is done and tested, work through this sequence:

  1. Document 2-3 more high-priority processes using the templates
  2. Customize for your industry using the Industry Customizer prompts
  3. Set up a review schedule — even great SOPs go stale. Quarterly reviews keep them useful.
  4. Build your SOP library using Industry Customizer Prompt 8 to plan your full documentation roadmap
