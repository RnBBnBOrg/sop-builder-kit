## 1. Purpose

Get every new hire from signed offer to fully productive team member — with nothing forgotten, no awkward first days, and a clear path to their first wins.

---

## 2. Scope

- **Applies to:** All new hires (full-time, part-time, contractors)
- **Triggers:** Accepted offer letter
- **Does NOT cover:** Recruiting, interviewing, or performance management

---

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Hiring Manager | Owns the onboarding plan, assigns buddy, runs 30-day check-in |
| Operations / Admin | Sets up systems, handles paperwork, orders equipment |
| Buddy / Mentor | Day-to-day support, answers "dumb questions," social integration |
| New Hire | Completes tasks, asks questions, communicates blockers |

---

## 4. Onboarding Timeline

| Timeframe | Phase | Focus |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Pre-Day 1 | Pre-boarding | Paperwork, equipment, access setup |
| Day 1 | Welcome Day | Orientation, introductions, workspace setup |
| Week 1 | Foundation | Tools, processes, culture, first small task |
| Week 2-4 | Ramp-Up | Increasing ownership, regular check-ins |
| Day 30 | Check-In | Feedback exchange, adjust expectations |
| Day 60 | Milestone | Independent contributor, broader context |
| Day 90 | Full Speed | Fully productive, formal review |

---

## 5. Procedure

### Phase 1: Pre-Boarding (Before Day 1)

**Who:** Operations / Admin + Hiring Manager

**1-2 weeks before start date:**

- [ ] Send welcome email with:
  - Start date, time, and location (or remote login instructions)
  - What to bring (ID, documents for I-9/tax forms)
  - Dress code / what to expect
  - Parking / building access info
  - Name and contact of their Day 1 greeter
- [ ] Collect new hire paperwork:
  - [ ] Tax forms (W-4 / W-9)
  - [ ] Direct deposit information
  - [ ] Emergency contact
  - [ ] NDA / IP agreement (if applicable)
  - [ ] Employee handbook acknowledgment
- [ ] Order / prepare equipment:
  - [ ] Laptop / computer
  - [ ] Monitor, keyboard, mouse (if applicable)
  - [ ] Phone / headset
  - [ ] Office supplies
- [ ] Set up accounts:
  - [ ] Email
  - [ ] Slack / Teams
  - [ ] Project management tool
  - [ ] Shared drive access
  - [ ] Industry-specific tools
  - [ ] Time tracking / HR system
- [ ] Assign a buddy/mentor
- [ ] Prepare Day 1 agenda
- [ ] Announce new hire to the team (with a short bio and role)

---

### Phase 2: Day 1 — Welcome

**Who:** Hiring Manager + Buddy

**Morning:**
- [ ] Greet new hire (in person or video call)
- [ ] Give office tour (or virtual workspace tour — where things live)
- [ ] Walk through Day 1 agenda together
- [ ] Verify all accounts and access work
- [ ] Complete any remaining paperwork

**Midday:**
- [ ] Team lunch or virtual coffee with the team
- [ ] Introductions — who does what, who to go to for what

**Afternoon:**
- [ ] Walk through the company overview:
  - What the company does and why it matters
  - Who the customers are
  - How their role fits into the bigger picture
- [ ] Review communication norms:
  - When to use email vs. Slack vs. calls
  - Meeting culture (camera on/off, agendas, notes)
  - Response time expectations
- [ ] Assign first small task (something they can complete today or tomorrow — an early win)

**End of Day:**
- [ ] 15-minute check-in: "How was Day 1? Any questions? Anything you need?"

---

### Phase 3: Week 1 — Foundation

**Who:** Hiring Manager + Buddy

- [ ] Daily 15-minute check-ins (can reduce after Week 1)
- [ ] Walk through key processes and SOPs for their role
- [ ] Introduce them to key cross-functional contacts
- [ ] Provide access to training materials or resources
- [ ] Shadow a team member on a real task or meeting
- [ ] Assign 2-3 real tasks with clear expectations and deadlines
- [ ] End-of-week check-in (30 min):
  - What went well?
  - What's confusing?
  - What do they need?

---

### Phase 4: Weeks 2-4 — Ramp-Up

**Who:** Hiring Manager

- [ ] Shift from daily to 2x/week check-ins
- [ ] Increase task complexity and ownership
- [ ] Include them in team meetings and planning
- [ ] Introduce broader company context (other teams, strategy, goals)
- [ ] Encourage them to update or create an SOP for something they learned
- [ ] Buddy continues to be available for questions

---

### Phase 5: 30-Day Check-In

**Who:** Hiring Manager + New Hire

Structured conversation (30-45 min):

| Topic | Questions |
|-------|-----------|
| **Role Clarity** | "Is your role what you expected? Any surprises?" |
| **Support** | "Do you have what you need to do your job? Tools, info, access?" |
| **Culture** | "How are you feeling about the team and company culture?" |
| **Feedback** | "What feedback do you have for us on the onboarding process?" |
| **Goals** | "Let's set 2-3 clear goals for the next 30 days." |

- [ ] Document the conversation and any action items
- [ ] Adjust onboarding plan if needed

---

### Phase 6: 60-Day Milestone

**Who:** Hiring Manager

- [ ] Check-in on goals set at Day 30
- [ ] Expand responsibilities if ready
- [ ] Reduce check-in frequency to weekly or as-needed
- [ ] Solicit feedback from team members who work with the new hire

---

### Phase 7: 90-Day Review

**Who:** Hiring Manager

- [ ] Formal performance check-in
- [ ] Review against role expectations
- [ ] Discuss career development and growth areas
- [ ] Confirm: are they set up for long-term success?
- [ ] Close out the onboarding process formally

---

## 6. New Hire Welcome Kit Checklist

Give them everything they need in one place:

- [ ] Company overview / mission / values one-pager
- [ ] Org chart — who's who
- [ ] Key contacts list (IT help, HR, their manager, their buddy)
- [ ] List of tools and login instructions
- [ ] Link to SOPs and process documentation
- [ ] FAQ: "Things I wish I knew on Day 1"
- [ ] Swag (if applicable — branded notebook, mug, stickers)

---

## 7. Red Flags to Watch For

| Red Flag | Response |
|----------|----------|
| New hire seems disengaged or overwhelmed in Week 1 | Have buddy check in informally; reduce task load |
| Accounts or equipment not ready on Day 1 | This is an ops failure — fix immediately and apologize |
| No questions being asked | Either overconfident or afraid to ask — buddy should proactively check in |
| Negative feedback at 30-day check-in | Take it seriously, adjust, follow up at Day 45 |

---

## 8. Quality Checkpoints

- [ ] All accounts and equipment ready before Day 1
- [ ] New hire received welcome email before start date
- [ ] Day 1 agenda completed in full
- [ ] Week 1 check-in completed
- [ ] 30-day, 60-day, and 90-day check-ins completed
- [ ] New hire confirms they feel set up for success (verbal or survey)
