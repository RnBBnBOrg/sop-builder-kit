## 1. Purpose

Catch mistakes before your clients do. This SOP defines how work gets reviewed, approved, and released — so every deliverable meets the standard your reputation depends on.

---

## 2. Scope

- **Applies to:** All client-facing deliverables and internal outputs that impact operations
- **Triggers:** Any work product moving from "done" to "delivered" or "published"
- **Does NOT cover:** Internal brainstorms, rough drafts, or work-in-progress (QA starts when the creator says "this is ready for review")

---

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Creator | Produces the work, runs self-check before submitting for review |
| Reviewer | Evaluates against quality criteria, provides specific feedback |
| Approver | Final sign-off — confirms it's ready to ship |
| Client Contact (if applicable) | Presents the deliverable, collects client feedback |

---

## 4. Quality Levels

Not everything needs the same level of review. Match the review depth to the risk:

| Level | When to Use | Review Process |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| **Quick Check** | Low-risk, internal, or routine work | Self-check + one peer review |
| **Standard Review** | Client deliverables, published content | Self-check + reviewer + approver |
| **Deep Review** | High-stakes, complex, or first-of-its-kind work | Self-check + reviewer + approver + second opinion |

---

## 5. Procedure

### Step 1: Creator Self-Check

**Who:** The person who produced the work
**When:** Before submitting for any review

Run through the **Self-Check Checklist** (customize per deliverable type):

**Universal Self-Check:**
- [ ] Does this meet the requirements in the brief / ticket / request?
- [ ] Have I proofread for spelling, grammar, and formatting?
- [ ] Are all names, numbers, and dates correct?
- [ ] Does this match our brand standards (voice, visual identity)?
- [ ] Have I tested all links, buttons, or interactive elements?
- [ ] Would I be comfortable if the CEO / biggest client saw this?
- [ ] Am I submitting my best work, or rushing to hit a deadline?

**Type-Specific Self-Checks:**

*Documents & Reports:*
- [ ] Page numbers, headers, footers correct
- [ ] Table of contents matches actual sections
- [ ] Charts and data are current and sourced
- [ ] Client's name and details are correct throughout (no copy-paste from another client)

*Design / Creative:*
- [ ] Correct dimensions for the target platform
- [ ] Brand colors, fonts, and logo used correctly
- [ ] Text is readable at actual display size
- [ ] High-resolution assets used (no pixelation)

*Code / Technical:*
- [ ] Runs without errors
- [ ] Tested against requirements / acceptance criteria
- [ ] Edge cases considered
- [ ] No hardcoded credentials or sensitive data

---

### Step 2: Submit for Review

**Who:** Creator
**When:** After self-check is complete

- [ ] Mark the work as "Ready for Review" in your project management tool
- [ ] Tag the assigned reviewer
- [ ] Include:
  - Link to the deliverable
  - The original brief or requirements
  - Any context the reviewer needs ("client is sensitive about X," "this is a new format for us")
  - Deadline for review completion

---

### Step 3: Reviewer Evaluation

**Who:** Reviewer
**When:** Within the agreed review window (default: 1 business day for Quick Check, 2 for Standard/Deep)

**Review Checklist:**
- [ ] **Accuracy** — Are all facts, figures, and references correct?
- [ ] **Completeness** — Does it cover everything in the brief?
- [ ] **Consistency** — Does it match our standards and prior work?
- [ ] **Clarity** — Will the audience understand it without explanation?
- [ ] **Quality** — Is this something we're proud to put our name on?

**Providing Feedback:**
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Be specific ("The chart on page 3 uses 2024 data — update to 2025") | Be vague ("Fix the data") |
| Explain why ("The client specifically asked for monthly breakdown") | Just say "wrong" |
| Distinguish must-fix from nice-to-have | Treat everything as critical |
| Acknowledge what's done well | Only point out problems |

**After reviewing:**
- [ ] Mark as "Approved," "Needs Revisions," or "Needs Discussion"
- [ ] If revisions needed: list specific changes required
- [ ] If discussion needed: schedule a 15-minute call rather than going back and forth in comments

---

### Step 4: Revisions (if needed)

**Who:** Creator
**When:** Within 1 business day of receiving feedback

- [ ] Address all required changes
- [ ] Respond to each piece of feedback (what you changed, or why you didn't)
- [ ] Resubmit for review
- [ ] If this is the second round of revisions, escalate to the Approver for a final call

---

### Step 5: Final Approval

**Who:** Approver
**When:** After reviewer marks as "Approved" (or after revisions are accepted)

- [ ] Quick final review — focus on overall quality and brand alignment
- [ ] Approve for delivery / publication
- [ ] If not approved: provide a specific reason and required action

---

### Step 6: Delivery / Release

**Who:** Client Contact or Publisher

- [ ] Deliver to client or publish per the relevant SOP
- [ ] Confirm receipt / successful publication
- [ ] Document any client feedback for future improvement

---

## 6. Common Quality Issues & Prevention

| Issue | Root Cause | Prevention |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| Wrong client name in deliverable | Copy-paste from previous project | Add "client name check" to self-check |
| Outdated data or statistics | Reusing old content without updating | Always verify dates on data sources |
| Broken links | Links changed after document was created | Check all links before final approval |
| Inconsistent formatting | Multiple contributors, no style guide | Create and enforce a style guide |
| Missed requirements | Brief not read carefully | Reviewer checks deliverable against brief line by line |

---

## 7. Escalation Path

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Creator and reviewer disagree | Approver makes the call |
| Quality issue found after delivery | Notify client immediately, fix, and do a post-mortem |
| Deadline conflict prevents proper review | Escalate to Approver — never skip review to hit a deadline |
| Recurring quality issues from same source | Hiring Manager addresses in 1:1, not in review comments |

---

## 8. Metrics to Track

| Metric | Target | How to Measure |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| First-pass approval rate | >70% | Deliverables approved without revisions / total deliverables |
| Average revision rounds | <2 | Track in project management tool |
| Client revision requests | <1 per deliverable | Count client-requested changes after delivery |
| Review turnaround time | <2 business days | Time from "Ready for Review" to feedback |

---

## 9. Quality Checkpoints

- [ ] Creator completed self-check before submitting
- [ ] Reviewer evaluated within the agreed window
- [ ] All required revisions addressed
- [ ] Final approval received before delivery
- [ ] No quality issues reported by client within 5 business days
