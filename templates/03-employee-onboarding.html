1. Purpose

Get every new hire from signed offer to fully productive team member — with nothing forgotten, no awkward first days, and a clear path to their first wins.

2. Scope

  • Applies to: All new hires (full-time, part-time, contractors)
  • Triggers: Accepted offer letter
  • Does NOT cover: Recruiting, interviewing, or performance management

3. Roles & Responsibilities

RoleResponsibility
Hiring ManagerOwns the onboarding plan, assigns buddy, runs 30-day check-in
Operations / AdminSets up systems, handles paperwork, orders equipment
Buddy / MentorDay-to-day support, answers "dumb questions," social integration
New HireCompletes tasks, asks questions, communicates blockers

4. Onboarding Timeline

TimeframePhaseFocus
Pre-Day 1Pre-boardingPaperwork, equipment, access setup
Day 1Welcome DayOrientation, introductions, workspace setup
Week 1FoundationTools, processes, culture, first small task
Week 2-4Ramp-UpIncreasing ownership, regular check-ins
Day 30Check-InFeedback exchange, adjust expectations
Day 60MilestoneIndependent contributor, broader context
Day 90Full SpeedFully productive, formal review

5. Procedure

Phase 1: Pre-Boarding (Before Day 1)

Who: Operations / Admin + Hiring Manager

1-2 weeks before start date:

  • Start date, time, and location (or remote login instructions)
  • What to bring (ID, documents for I-9/tax forms)
  • Dress code / what to expect
  • Parking / building access info
  • Name and contact of their Day 1 greeter

Phase 2: Day 1 — Welcome

Who: Hiring Manager + Buddy

Morning:

Midday:

Afternoon:

  • What the company does and why it matters
  • Who the customers are
  • How their role fits into the bigger picture
  • When to use email vs. Slack vs. calls
  • Meeting culture (camera on/off, agendas, notes)
  • Response time expectations

End of Day:

Phase 3: Week 1 — Foundation

Who: Hiring Manager + Buddy

  • What went well?
  • What's confusing?
  • What do they need?

Phase 4: Weeks 2-4 — Ramp-Up

Who: Hiring Manager

Phase 5: 30-Day Check-In

Who: Hiring Manager + New Hire

Structured conversation (30-45 min):

TopicQuestions
Role Clarity"Is your role what you expected? Any surprises?"
Support"Do you have what you need to do your job? Tools, info, access?"
Culture"How are you feeling about the team and company culture?"
Feedback"What feedback do you have for us on the onboarding process?"
Goals"Let's set 2-3 clear goals for the next 30 days."

7. Red Flags to Watch For

Red FlagResponse
New hire seems disengaged or overwhelmed in Week 1Have buddy check in informally; reduce task load
Accounts or equipment not ready on Day 1This is an ops failure — fix immediately and apologize
No questions being askedEither overconfident or afraid to ask — buddy should proactively check in
Negative feedback at 30-day check-inTake it seriously, adjust, follow up at Day 45
