## 1. Purpose

Ensure every new client has a consistent, professional onboarding experience — from signed contract to first deliverable — so nothing falls through the cracks and the client feels confident they made the right choice.

---

## 2. Scope

- **Applies to:** All new client engagements
- **Triggers:** Signed contract or accepted proposal
- **Does NOT cover:** Pre-sales, proposals, or ongoing account management

---

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Account Owner | Primary client relationship, oversees onboarding |
| Operations / Admin | Sets up systems, sends welcome materials |
| Delivery Lead | Plans first deliverable, runs kickoff |
| Finance | Sends invoice, confirms payment setup |

---

## 4. Onboarding Timeline

| Day | Milestone |
|-----|-----------|
| Day 0 | Contract signed — trigger onboarding |
| Day 0-1 | Welcome email + intake form sent |
| Day 1-2 | Internal setup (project, channels, folders) |
| Day 3-5 | Intake form received, reviewed |
| Day 5-7 | Kickoff call scheduled and held |
| Day 7-10 | First deliverable or milestone started |
| Day 14 | Check-in call — "How's it going?" |

---

## 5. Procedure

### Phase 1: Welcome (Day 0-1)

**Who:** Account Owner + Operations

- [ ] Send **Welcome Email** within 24 hours of signed contract
  - Thank them for choosing you
  - Introduce their main point of contact
  - Attach or link the **Client Intake Form**
  - Set expectation for kickoff call timing
- [ ] Send **intake form** (see template below)
- [ ] Add client to your CRM / project management tool
- [ ] Create client folder in shared drive

**Welcome Email Template:**
> Subject: Welcome to [Your Company] — Let's Get Started
>
> Hi [Client Name],
>
> We're excited to work with you! Here's what happens next:
>
> 1. **Fill out the intake form** (linked below) — takes about 10 minutes
> 2. **We'll schedule a kickoff call** within the next 5 business days
> 3. **You'll meet your delivery team** and we'll align on priorities
>
> Intake Form: [Link]
>
> Your main point of contact is [Name] ([email]). Don't hesitate to reach out with any questions.
>
> Looking forward to getting started!

---

### Phase 2: Internal Setup (Day 1-2)

**Who:** Operations

- [ ] Create project in project management tool (Notion, Asana, Monday, etc.)
- [ ] Set up communication channel (Slack channel, Teams group, or email thread)
- [ ] Create shared folder structure:
  ```
  [Client Name]/
  ├── 01-Contract & Agreements/
  ├── 02-Intake & Discovery/
  ├── 03-Deliverables/
  ├── 04-Communications/
  └── 05-Invoices & Billing/
  ```
- [ ] Add client to billing/invoicing system
- [ ] Send first invoice (if applicable)

---

### Phase 3: Discovery & Intake Review (Day 3-5)

**Who:** Account Owner + Delivery Lead

- [ ] Review completed intake form
- [ ] Note any gaps or follow-up questions
- [ ] Research client's business (website, social media, competitors)
- [ ] Prepare kickoff agenda (see template below)
- [ ] Schedule kickoff call — send calendar invite with agenda attached

**Intake Form — Key Questions:**

| Category | Questions |
|----------|-----------|
| **Business Basics** | Company name, industry, size, website |
| **Goals** | What does success look like in 30/60/90 days? |
| **Pain Points** | What's not working right now? What prompted this engagement? |
| **Stakeholders** | Who are the decision-makers? Who will we work with day-to-day? |
| **Preferences** | Communication preference (email/Slack/calls)? Meeting frequency? |
| **Access & Logins** | What systems/tools do we need access to? |
| **Past Experience** | Have you worked with a company like ours before? What went well/poorly? |
| **Budget & Timeline** | Any hard deadlines or budget constraints beyond the contract? |

---

### Phase 4: Kickoff Call (Day 5-7)

**Who:** Account Owner + Delivery Lead + Client

**Kickoff Agenda (45-60 min):**

1. **(5 min)** Introductions — who's who on both sides
2. **(10 min)** Confirm goals and success metrics
3. **(10 min)** Review intake form answers — fill in gaps
4. **(10 min)** Walk through the project plan / first 30 days
5. **(5 min)** Communication norms — how and when we talk
6. **(5 min)** Tools & access — confirm everything is set up
7. **(5 min)** Q&A and next steps

**After the kickoff:**
- [ ] Send kickoff summary email within 24 hours
- [ ] Include: confirmed goals, agreed timeline, next steps, action items
- [ ] Update project plan based on any changes from the call

---

### Phase 5: First Deliverable (Day 7-10)

**Who:** Delivery Lead

- [ ] Begin work on first deliverable or milestone
- [ ] Share progress update with client midway
- [ ] Deliver first output with context (not just a file — explain what they're looking at)
- [ ] Ask for initial feedback

---

### Phase 6: Check-In (Day 14)

**Who:** Account Owner

- [ ] Schedule a 15-minute check-in call
- [ ] Ask:
  - "How are you feeling about things so far?"
  - "Is communication working well for you?"
  - "Anything we should adjust?"
- [ ] Document feedback internally
- [ ] Adjust process if needed
- [ ] Transition to ongoing account management rhythm

---

## 6. Red Flags to Watch For

| Red Flag | Response |
|----------|----------|
| Client hasn't returned intake form after 3 days | Send a friendly follow-up; offer to do it over a call instead |
| Stakeholder conflict or unclear decision-maker | Address directly in kickoff — "Who has final say on X?" |
| Scope creep in kickoff | Note it, don't agree on the spot — "Let's add that to the roadmap" |
| Client is unresponsive after kickoff | Escalate to Account Owner for a direct outreach |

## 8. Quality Checkpoints

- [ ] Client received welcome email within 24 hours
- [ ] All internal systems set up before kickoff
- [ ] Kickoff call held within 7 days of contract signing
- [ ] First deliverable shipped within 10 days
- [ ] Check-in call completed at Day 14
- [ ] Client confirmed satisfaction (verbal or written)
