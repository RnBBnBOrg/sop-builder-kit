1. Purpose

Take content from raw idea to published and promoted — with consistent quality, clear approvals, and nothing sitting in draft purgatory forever.

2. Scope

  • Applies to: All published content (blog posts, social media, newsletters, case studies, videos)
  • Triggers: Approved content idea added to the content calendar
  • Does NOT cover: Paid advertising copy, legal/compliance documents, or internal-only communications

3. Roles & Responsibilities

RoleResponsibility
Content Lead / ManagerOwns the calendar, assigns work, final approval
Writer / CreatorProduces the draft, incorporates feedback
Editor / ReviewerReviews for quality, accuracy, brand voice
Designer (if applicable)Creates visuals, graphics, thumbnails
PublisherFormats, schedules, and publishes the final piece

In a small team, one person may fill multiple roles.

5. Procedure

Stage 1: Idea Capture

Who: Anyone on the team

  • Working title
  • Target audience
  • Key message or angle
  • Content type (blog, social, newsletter, video, etc.)
  • Source of idea (customer question, trend, internal expertise, keyword research)

Idea Evaluation Criteria:

CriteriaQuestion
RelevanceDoes our audience care about this?
ExpertiseCan we say something credible and useful?
TimelinessIs this relevant now or evergreen?
Business AlignmentDoes this support our goals (leads, authority, SEO)?
