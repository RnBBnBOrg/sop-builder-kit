AI Prompt Pack: SOP Generator

Use these prompts to generate complete, ready-to-use SOPs with any AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.). Copy the prompt, fill in your details, and get a professional SOP in minutes.

Prompt 1: Generate a Complete SOP from Scratch

Best for creating a new SOP when you know the process but haven't documented it yet.

I need you to create a complete Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for my business.

Business context:
- Business type: [e.g., "marketing agency," "property management company," "e-commerce brand"]
- Team size: [e.g., "5 people," "just me," "15-person team"]
- Process to document: [e.g., "client onboarding," "content publishing," "order fulfillment"]

Here's how the process currently works (in plain English):
[Describe the process step by step as you'd explain it to a new employee. Don't worry about formatting — just explain what happens, in what order, and who does it.]

Create an SOP that includes:
1. Purpose — why this process exists (1-2 sentences)
2. Scope — what triggers this process and what it covers
3. Roles — who's involved and what they're responsible for
4. Step-by-step procedure — clear, numbered steps with enough detail that someone new could follow them
5. Quality checkpoints — where to pause and verify before moving forward
6. Common mistakes and how to avoid them
7. Metrics to track — how to know if this process is working well

Format it professionally with clear headings. Write it for someone who's smart but has never done this before.

Prompt 2: Convert Messy Notes into a Clean SOP

Best when you have scattered notes, checklists, or Slack messages about a process that needs organizing.

I have messy notes about a process that I need turned into a professional SOP. Here are my raw notes:

[Paste your notes, bullet points, Slack messages, checklists, or any documentation you have — messy is fine]

Business context:
- This process is for: [what team/department]
- It happens: [how often — daily, weekly, per client, per project, etc.]
- The person doing this is typically: [role/title]

Turn this into a clean, professional SOP with:
1. Purpose statement
2. Clear scope (when this applies, when it doesn't)
3. Roles and responsibilities
4. Numbered step-by-step procedure (organize my notes into logical order)
5. Quality checkpoints
6. Any warnings or common mistakes you can infer from the notes

Fill in gaps where my notes are incomplete — mark anything you assumed with [ASSUMPTION — verify this]. Keep the tone professional but approachable.

Prompt 3: Create SOPs from a Job Description

Best when you're hiring for a role and want to document the key processes before the new person starts.

I'm hiring for this role and I want to create SOPs for their core responsibilities before they start.

Here's the job description:
[Paste the job description]

Create SOPs for the top [3-5] most important processes this person will handle. For each SOP, include:
1. Purpose
2. Step-by-step procedure
3. Quality standards — what "done well" looks like
4. Common mistakes to avoid
5. Who to ask for help

Keep each SOP to one page. These are for a new hire who's competent but unfamiliar with how we do things specifically.

Prompt 4: Generate an SOP from a Recorded Process

Best when you have a Loom transcript, meeting recording, or video walkthrough of someone doing the process.

I have a transcript of someone walking through a business process. I need you to convert it into a clean, professional SOP.

Here is the transcript:
[Paste the transcript or detailed summary of the recording]

Context:
- Process name: [what this process is called]
- Who recorded it: [their role]
- Audience: [who will follow this SOP]

Convert this into a structured SOP with:
1. Purpose
2. Required tools/access/accounts needed before starting
3. Step-by-step procedure (extract the actual steps from the conversational transcript)
4. Screenshots or visual markers — note where a screenshot would be helpful with [SCREENSHOT: description of what to capture]
5. Quality check — how to verify the process was done correctly
6. Troubleshooting — note any issues or edge cases mentioned in the recording

Remove all conversational filler, tangents, and "um"s. Keep only the actionable content.

Prompt 5: Build an Emergency / Escalation SOP

Best for documenting what to do when things go wrong — outages, complaints, security incidents.

I need an emergency/escalation SOP for my business.

Business type: [e.g., "SaaS company," "rental property management," "consulting firm"]
Scenario to document: [e.g., "website goes down," "client escalation," "data breach," "negative review goes viral," "key employee quits mid-project"]

Create an SOP that covers:
1. How to identify and classify the severity of this situation (levels 1-3)
2. Immediate response — what to do in the first 15 minutes for each severity level
3. Communication plan — who to notify, in what order, what to say
4. Resolution steps — how to fix or contain the situation
5. Follow-up — what to do after the crisis is resolved (post-mortem, client communication, prevention)
6. Contact list template — who to call for what

Include specific message templates for communicating with:
- Affected clients/customers
- Internal team
- Vendors/partners (if relevant)

Write it so someone could follow this under pressure at 2am without needing to think too hard.

Prompt 6: Create a Training SOP for Delegating a Task

Best when you currently do something yourself and want to hand it off to someone else.

I currently handle [task/process] myself and I want to delegate it. I need an SOP that's detailed enough for someone to take over completely.

Here's what I do:
[Describe the task in your own words — include the tools you use, how you make decisions, what "good" looks like, and any shortcuts or tricks you've developed]

The person taking over:
- Role: [e.g., "VA," "new hire," "team member"]
- Experience level: [e.g., "beginner," "knows the tools but not our process," "experienced but new to our company"]
- Access needed: [list any accounts, tools, or permissions they'll need]

Create an SOP that:
1. Explains the purpose — why we do this and why it matters
2. Lists prerequisites — tools, access, and knowledge needed
3. Walks through the process step by step (assume no prior knowledge of our way of doing things)
4. Includes decision trees for common judgment calls (if X, then do Y)
5. Defines quality standards — what to check before marking it done
6. Lists common mistakes and how to recover from them
7. Specifies when to ask for help vs. make a judgment call

Add a "first week" checklist: a simplified version for the first few times they do it, with extra checkpoints.

Prompt 7: Generate a Recurring Process SOP (Daily/Weekly/Monthly)

Best for documenting processes that happen on a regular schedule.

I need an SOP for a recurring process in my business.

Process: [e.g., "weekly client reporting," "daily social media posting," "monthly bookkeeping close"]
Frequency: [daily / weekly / bi-weekly / monthly / quarterly]
Who does it: [role/title]
Tools used: [list software, platforms, spreadsheets, etc.]

Here's what the process involves:
[Describe what gets done each cycle]

Create an SOP that includes:
1. Purpose — why this recurs and what depends on it
2. Schedule — when it starts, when it's due, any dependencies on other processes
3. Pre-work checklist — what to gather/verify before starting
4. Step-by-step procedure for each cycle
5. Quality check — how to verify this cycle's work before moving on
6. Handoff — who gets the output and in what format
7. Exception handling — what to do when you miss a cycle, data is late, or something breaks

Include a one-line calendar reminder I can copy-paste for scheduling this.
