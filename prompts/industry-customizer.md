# Industry Customizer — AI Prompt Pack

Use these prompts to adapt any generic SOP template to your specific industry, adding the terminology, compliance requirements, and best practices that make it actually useful for your business.

---

## Prompt 1: Full Industry Adaptation

```
I have a generic SOP template that I want to customize for my specific industry. Take this template and adapt it by:

1. **Terminology** — Replace generic terms with industry-specific language my team actually uses
2. **Compliance** — Add any regulatory or compliance steps required in my industry
3. **Best practices** — Include industry-specific best practices and standards
4. **Common pitfalls** — Add warnings about mistakes that are common in my industry
5. **Tools** — Suggest industry-standard tools for each step (if I haven't specified mine)

My industry: [YOUR INDUSTRY — e.g., healthcare, legal, real estate, marketing agency, restaurant, retail, construction, accounting, fitness, SaaS]

My business size: [NUMBER OF EMPLOYEES]

My role: [YOUR ROLE]

Any specific regulations we follow: [e.g., HIPAA, GDPR, SOX, PCI-DSS, OSHA, or "none that I know of"]

The template to customize:

[PASTE ANY SOP TEMPLATE FROM THIS KIT]
```

```
2. **Documentation requirements** — what records we need to keep and for how long
3. **Audit trail** — what needs to be logged, tracked, or signed off

5. **Penalties for non-compliance** — what's at risk if we skip these steps

If I said I'm not sure what regulations apply, first tell me which regulations likely apply to my industry and business type, then add the relevant compliance steps.

Existing SOP:

[PASTE YOUR SOP]
```

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Name your industry AND sub-niche.** "Marketing" is broad. "B2B SaaS content marketing agency, 8 people" gets you specific, usable output.
2. **Mention regulations even if you're not sure.** Say "I think HIPAA applies but I'm not sure what else" — the AI will fill in the gaps.
3. **Stack prompts.** Use the SOP Generator first to create a base SOP, then run it through the Industry Customizer to tailor it.
4. **Validate compliance additions with a professional.** AI is a great starting point for compliance, but always verify regulatory requirements with a qualified professional in your industry.
5. **Save your customized versions.** Once you've adapted a template for your industry, that becomes YOUR template — reuse it for future SOPs instead of starting from generic each time.
