AI Prompt Pack: Process Auditor

Use these prompts to review, improve, and stress-test your existing SOPs. Paste your current SOP into the prompt and get actionable feedback.

Prompt 1: Full SOP Audit

Best for a comprehensive review of an existing SOP. Identifies gaps, ambiguity, and improvement opportunities.

I need you to audit this Standard Operating Procedure. Review it as if you're a new employee trying to follow it for the first time.

Here is the SOP:
[Paste your complete SOP]

Evaluate it on:
1. **Clarity** — Could someone new follow this without asking questions? Flag any vague steps.
2. **Completeness** — Are there missing steps, unstated assumptions, or gaps where someone could get stuck?
3. **Logic** — Are the steps in the right order? Are there dependencies that aren't mentioned?
4. **Decision points** — Where does someone need to make a judgment call? Are the criteria clear?
5. **Error handling** — What could go wrong at each step? Is there guidance for recovering from mistakes?
6. **Measurability** — How would a manager know if this process is being followed correctly?

For each issue you find:
- Quote the specific part of the SOP
- Explain the problem
- Suggest a specific fix (rewrite the section, don't just say "make it clearer")

Rate the SOP overall: Ready to Use / Needs Minor Edits / Needs Major Revision / Start Over

Prompt 2: Identify Automation Opportunities

Best for finding steps that could be automated, templated, or eliminated.

I want to find automation and efficiency opportunities in this SOP.

Here is the SOP:
[Paste your SOP]

Tools we currently use: [e.g., "Google Workspace, Slack, Notion, Zapier, QuickBooks"]
Budget for new tools: [e.g., "up to $50/month" or "prefer free tools" or "open to suggestions"]

For each step in the SOP, tell me:
1. Can this step be fully automated? (If yes, how — specific tool + setup)
2. Can this step be partially automated? (If yes, what part stays manual?)
3. Can this step be eliminated entirely? (If yes, why it's unnecessary)
4. Can this step be templated? (If yes, create the template)

Then give me a prioritized list:
- Quick wins (under 1 hour to set up, immediate time savings)
- Medium effort (1-4 hours to set up, significant time savings)
- Big projects (more than 4 hours, but worth it for the long-term)

Estimate the time saved per week/month for each automation.

Prompt 3: Stress-Test Your SOP

Best for finding edge cases and failure modes before they happen in real life.

I need you to stress-test this SOP by imagining realistic scenarios where it could break down.

Here is the SOP:
[Paste your SOP]

Business context: [brief description of your business and who follows this SOP]

Generate 10 realistic scenarios that would test this SOP, including:
- A step where the responsible person is unavailable
- A situation where the tools/software mentioned are down
- A case where the inputs or data are incomplete
- A time-sensitive situation where the normal timeline can't be followed
- An edge case that doesn't fit neatly into the documented process
- A scenario where two steps conflict with each other
- A situation involving an unhappy client or stakeholder

For each scenario:
1. Describe what happens
2. Identify where the SOP fails or is unclear
3. Suggest a specific addition or change to handle it

Rank the scenarios by likelihood (most likely to actually happen first).

Prompt 4: Simplify an Overcomplicated SOP

Best when your SOP is too long, too detailed, or nobody actually follows it because it's overwhelming.

This SOP is too complicated and people aren't following it. I need you to simplify it without losing the important parts.

Here is the SOP:
[Paste your SOP]

Context:
- Who follows this: [role/experience level]
- How often: [frequency]
- Main complaint about this SOP: [e.g., "too many steps," "too much detail," "takes too long to read"]

Simplify it by:
1. Merging steps that are really one action
2. Removing steps that are obvious to someone at this experience level
3. Converting paragraph instructions into bullet points or checklists
4. Moving edge cases and exceptions to an appendix (keep the main SOP clean)
5. Creating a "quick reference" version (one page, just the key steps) alongside the full version

Give me:
- The simplified SOP (aim for 50% fewer words)
- A one-page quick reference card
- A list of what you removed and why (so I can verify nothing important was cut)

Prompt 5: Compare Two Versions of a Process

Best when you have a current process and a proposed change, and want to evaluate the tradeoffs.

I'm considering changing how we do something. Compare these two approaches and help me decide.

Current process:
[Paste current SOP or describe current approach]

Proposed process:
[Paste proposed SOP or describe proposed approach]

Context:
- Why I'm considering a change: [what problem prompted this]
- Team size: [number of people affected]
- Risk tolerance: [how bad would it be if the new process fails?]

Compare them on:
1. Efficiency — time per cycle
2. Quality — error rate and consistency
3. Scalability — how well each handles 2x, 5x, 10x volume
4. Complexity — how hard each is to learn and follow
5. Dependencies — tools, people, or systems each requires
6. Risk — what could go wrong with each

Give me a clear recommendation with:
- Which to use and why
- What to keep from the current process even if switching
- A transition plan if you recommend switching (don't just flip a switch)
- Rollback criteria — when to switch back if it's not working

Prompt 6: Create KPIs for an Existing SOP

Best when you have a process that works but you can't measure whether it's working well.

I need to create KPIs and metrics for this SOP so I can track whether it's being followed and whether it's effective.

Here is the SOP:
[Paste your SOP]

Context:
- Business goal this SOP supports: [e.g., "client satisfaction," "revenue growth," "operational efficiency"]
- Current pain points: [e.g., "don't know if people are following it," "quality varies," "takes too long"]
- Tools available for tracking: [e.g., "Google Sheets, project management tool, CRM"]

Create:
1. 3-5 KPIs that measure the effectiveness of this process (not just whether steps were completed)
2. For each KPI: what to measure, target value, how to measure it, and how often
3. A simple dashboard or tracking template I can implement this week
4. Warning signs — specific metrics that indicate the process is breaking down
5. A quarterly review checklist for evaluating and improving this SOP based on the data

Prompt 7: Update an Outdated SOP

Best when you have an SOP that was written a while ago and doesn't match how things actually work now.

I have an SOP that's outdated. The process has evolved but the documentation hasn't kept up. I need to update it.

Here is the original SOP:
[Paste your outdated SOP]

Here's what has changed since this was written:
[Describe changes — new tools, different team structure, modified steps, things that were added or removed, workarounds people use that aren't documented]

Update the SOP to reflect how the process actually works now. Specifically:
1. Update all steps to match current reality
2. Remove steps that are no longer relevant
3. Add steps that are being done but weren't documented
4. Update tool references, role titles, and any other stale information
5. Add a "what changed" section at the top so people who knew the old version can quickly see the differences
6. Update the revision history

Flag anything where my description of "what changed" seems inconsistent or where I might be missing something.
