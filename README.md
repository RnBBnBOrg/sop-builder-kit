# SOP Builder Kit — AI-Powered Standard Operating Procedures for Small Businesses

**Stop reinventing the wheel every time you document a process.**

The SOP Builder Kit gives you ready-to-use templates and AI prompts that turn your messy, undocumented workflows into clear, repeatable SOPs — in under 30 minutes each.

---

## What's Inside

### Interactive SOP Templates
Five battle-tested HTML templates that work directly in your browser — fill in fields, check boxes, and your work saves automatically:

| Template | What It Covers |
|----------|---------------|
| **General SOP** | Universal template that works for any process |
| **Client Onboarding** | First impression to full engagement — intake, kickoff, handoff |
| **Employee Onboarding** | New hire from offer letter to productive team member |
| **Content Publishing** | Idea to published — planning, drafting, review, distribution |
| **Quality Assurance** | Checklists, review gates, and sign-off workflows |

### AI Prompt Packs
Three prompt collections that do the heavy lifting:

| Prompt Pack | What It Does |
|-------------|-------------|
| **SOP Generator** | Feed it a rough process description, get a structured SOP back |
| **Process Auditor** | Analyze existing workflows to find gaps, bottlenecks, and risks |
| **Industry Customizer** | Adapt any template for your specific industry (healthcare, legal, agency, retail, etc.) |

### Quick-Start Guide
Step-by-step instructions to go from download to documented in one sitting.

## How to Use It

1. **Pick a template** — start with the process that causes the most pain
2. **Run the AI prompts** — paste the SOP Generator prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI tool with your rough notes
3. **Customize** — use the Industry Customizer prompts to tailor it to your business
4. **Fill in the template** — open it in your browser, fill in the fields, and your work saves automatically
5. **Audit & improve** — run the Process Auditor prompts quarterly to keep SOPs current
